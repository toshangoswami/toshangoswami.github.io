Toshan Goswami is an incoming Computer Engineering student at the University of Michigan. He builds at the intersection of hardware and software — from computer vision pipelines to physical machines — and enjoys turning ideas into working prototypes. Recent projects include a radio-controlled car built from scratch, a computer vision system for tracking badminton gameplay, a wildfire spread simulator, and a crack-detection tool for aircraft surfaces.
